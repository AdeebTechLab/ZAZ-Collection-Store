Return Window

Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a size exchange or store credit. To start a return, message us on WhatsApp or through the Contact page with your order details and the reason for the return.

Condition for Returns

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with their original tags and packaging. Sale items and made-to-order pieces are final sale unless received damaged or defective.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item so we can arrange a replacement or refund at no extra cost to you.
